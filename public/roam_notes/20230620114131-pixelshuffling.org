#+title: PixelShuffling

* Resources
- [[https://paperswithcode.com/method/pixelshuffle][paperswithcode]]
* Information
- PixelShuffle is an operation used in super-resolution models to implement efficient sub-pixel convolutions with a stride of $1/r$. Specifically it rearranges elements in a tensor of shape $\left( *, C \times r^{2}, H, W \right)$ to a tensor of shape $\left( *, C, H \times r, W \times r \right)$
