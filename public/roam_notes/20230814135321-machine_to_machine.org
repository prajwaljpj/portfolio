#+title: machine-to-machine

* Introduction
Machine-to-machine or M2M is a any technology that enables networked devices to communicate information and perform actions automatically. one of the widely known machine-to-machine communication is [[id:67fdd00e-b992-4c28-b532-8a3a5180b003][telemetry]] used for transmitting operational data.
* Types
M2M can be either wired or wireless
