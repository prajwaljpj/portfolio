#+title: Speech quality evaluation metrics

Speech and audio quality evaluation metrics can be broadly classified into subjective and objective metrics.

*** Subjective Evaluation Metrics
Subjective evaluation involves human listeners to judge the quality of audio. Common methods include:

1. *Mean Opinion Score (MOS)*:
   - Listeners rate the audio quality on a scale, typically from 1 (bad) to 5 (excellent).
   - The final score is the arithmetic mean of all ratings.
   - *Formula*:
     \[
     \text{MOS} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{rating}_i
     \]
   - Where \( N \) is the number of listeners and \( \text{rating}_i \) is the individual score from each listener.

   #+begin_src python
   def calculate_mos(ratings):
       return sum(ratings) / len(ratings)
   #+end_src

*** Objective Evaluation Metrics
Objective metrics use algorithms to predict perceived audio quality, often correlating well with MOS.

1. *Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)*:
   - Measures the ratio of the desired signal to background noise.
   - *Formula*:
     \[
     \text{SNR} = 10 \log_{10} \left( \frac{P_{\text{signal}}}{P_{\text{noise}}} \right)
     \]
   - Can also be calculated in terms of time-domain signals:
     \[
     \text{SNR} = 10 \log_{10} \left( \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{N} s[n]^2}{\sum_{n=1}^{N} (s[n] - \hat{s}[n])^2}} \right)
     \]
   - Where \( s[n] \) is the original signal and \( \hat{s}[n] \) is the distorted signal.

   #+begin_src python
   import numpy as np

   def calculate_snr(original, distorted):
       signal_power = np.sum(original**2)
       noise_power = np.sum((original - distorted)**2)
       return 10 * np.log10(signal_power / noise_power)
   #+end_src

2. *Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality (PESQ)*:
   - A standardized objective measure that compares a reference signal with a distorted signal.
   - It uses a model of human auditory perception to calculate quality scores.

   Note: PESQ implementation can be complex; libraries are typically used.

3. *Short-Time Objective Intelligibility (STOI)*:
   - Measures the intelligibility of speech signals based on time-frequency analysis.
   - The method involves comparing the clean and degraded signals using a segmented approach.

   For implementation, you can use the =pystoi= library, which can be installed via pip.

   #+begin_src python
   from pystoi import stoi

   def calculate_stoi(clean_signal, noisy_signal, sample_rate):
       return stoi(clean_signal, noisy_signal, rate=sample_rate)
   #+end_src

*** Summary
- *MOS* and subjective measures provide listener-based quality assessments.
- *SNR*, *PESQ*, and *STOI* are widely used objective metrics, relying on mathematical calculations and signal processing techniques.
- The above Python implementations provide simple calculations for SNR and MOS; PESQ and STOI require more complex implementations or third-party libraries.
