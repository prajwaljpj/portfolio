#+title: spectral band
Spectral bands are parts of the electromagnetic spectrum of specific wavelengths, which can be filtered by a standard filter.
* Nuclear Physics
Spectral bands are referred to the emission of polyatomic systems. Each spectral line corresponds to one level in the atom splits in the molecules.
