#+title: AI design tips

Here's what many companies overlooked in their AI implementation: 🔍

1) Their entire data pipeline depends on a single AI provider 🥶
2) No failover systems in place 🤌
3) Missing status checks in their code 🥴
4) Zero degradation handling 🫠
5) No ability to DISABLE AI in any data pieline 🤯

But it doesn't have to be this way. 💡

As someone who's deeply rooted in AI systems across enterprises, here's your essential AI resilience checklist:
✅ Multi-Provider Strategy
Always integrate multiple AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere). When one goes down, your system stays up.
✅ Graceful Degradation
Your code should have optional AI features, not critical dependencies.
✅ Health Checks
Implement continuous AI service monitoring. Don't wait for your users to tell you something's wrong.
