#+title: AVIRIS
* Hyperspectral Image dataset
Airborne Visible / Infrared Imaging Spectrometer (AVIRIS)  hyperspectral sensor data (aviris.jpl.nasa.gov/) were acquired on June 12, 1992 over the Purdue University Agronomy farm northwest of West Lafayette and the surrounding area.
