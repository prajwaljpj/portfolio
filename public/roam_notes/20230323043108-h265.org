#+title: High Efficiency Video Coding

Also known as HEVC or H.265 MPEG-H Part 2.
HEVC uses both integer DCT and discrete sine transform (DST) with varied block sizes between 4×4 and 32×32.
The High Efficiency Image Format (HEIF) is based on HEVC.
