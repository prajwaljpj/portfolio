#+title: Long Tailed Data

Long-tailed data refers to a distribution where a large number of occurrences are found in a relatively small number of items (the "head"), while many other items exist with very few occurrences (the "tail"). This creates a long tail of infrequent items.

*Example:*

In a book sales dataset, a few bestsellers (the head) sell thousands of copies, while many niche books (the tail) sell only a few copies each. This results in a distribution where most sales come from a small number of titles, yet there is a large variety of titles with low sales.
