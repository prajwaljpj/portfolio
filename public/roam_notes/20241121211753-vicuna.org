#+title: Vicuna

[[https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685][Paper Source]]

- 8 categories:
  1. Writing
  2. Roleplay
  3. Extraction
  4. Reasoning
  5. Math
  6. Coding
  7. STEM
  8. Humanities/Social Science
- 10 questions for each category
- Generate answers from 5 ChatBots
  1. LLaMA
  2. Alpaca
  3. ChatGPT
  4. Bard
  5. Vicuna
- Used GPT-4 to rate quality based on:
  1. Relevance
  2. Helpfulness
  3. Accuracy
  4. Detail
- Results:
  1. They found GPT-4 to provide consistent scores but could also give detailed explanation for those scores
  2. Had higher agreement with humans (85%) than humans had among themselves (81%)
  3. Concluded that GPT-4's evaluation aligns closely with human evaluators
