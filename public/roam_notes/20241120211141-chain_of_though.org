#+title: Chain-of-Thought

Chain-of-thought (CoT) is a reasoning technique used in large language models (LLMs) to improve their performance on complex tasks by enabling them to verbalize their reasoning process step-by-step. This approach helps the model to break down problems into smaller, manageable components, leading to more accurate and coherent conclusions.

*Example:*

*Problem:* What is the total cost if 3 apples cost $1 each and 2 bananas cost $0.50 each?

*Chain of Thought Reasoning:*
1. Calculate the cost of apples:
   - 3 apples x $1/apple = $3.
2. Calculate the cost of bananas:
   - 2 bananas x $0.50/banana = $1.
3. Add the costs together:
   - Total cost = $3 (apples) + $1 (bananas) = $4.

*Final Answer:* The total cost is $4.

This systematic breakdown helps ensure the model arrives at the correct solution rather than jumping directly to an answer.
